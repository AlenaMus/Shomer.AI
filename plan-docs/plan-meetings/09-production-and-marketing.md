# Meeting 9 — Presentation Assets & Final Report

**Phase:** D · Presentation · **Status:** ⬜ not started · **Source:** `Shomer_AI_10_Meeting_Plan` §9

## 🎯 Goal
Turn the technical result into a winning story: a 60–90 s promo video, slide deck, a recorded demo
backup, and the final summary report. A viewer should grasp the value without seeing a line of code.

## 📋 Before
- Sign up for a video tool (e.g. RunwayML / Pika / Synthesia) and a music tool (SUNO), if used.
- Study promo videos of successful AI products (Notion AI, Perplexity, Linear).

- The video makes sense even without sound.
- The deck follows Pain → Solution → Demo → Proof → Ask.
- Backup demo exists locally **and** in the cloud (not internet-dependent on defense day).
- Tag **v1.1** "Marketing Ready".

## ⚠️ Risks
- Video tool underdelivers → Plan B: screen recording + voice-over + simple editing.
- Deck too long → 15 slides ≈ 10 minutes; short is marketing, long is a lecture.
